What We're Known For

Signature modern designs that look beautiful in real life, not just in photos.

Welcome to V.A.Y Studio, a modern floral studio creating fresh flower bouquets in Chicago. Our style is clean, elegant, and intentional — with carefully selected blooms, balanced color palettes, and details that feel truly personal.
