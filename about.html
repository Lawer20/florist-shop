Modern Bouquets in Chicago

Premium fresh blooms. Editorial design. Thoughtful florals for every occasion.

Welcome to V.A.Y Studio, a modern floral studio creating fresh flower bouquets in Chicago. Our style is clean, elegant, and intentional — with carefully selected blooms, balanced color palettes, and details that feel truly personal.

We work with premium seasonal flowers to craft bouquets that look beautiful in real life, not just in photos. Whether you're celebrating a birthday, anniversary, milestone, or simply want to brighten someone's day, each arrangement is designed to feel special — refined, modern, and gift-ready.

Weddings, Events & Corporate Gifting: We create custom florals for weddings, celebrations, and corporate gifting across Chicago — from intimate moments to larger statements.
